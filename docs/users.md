# Users

## 1. Rental / Operations Manager

### Main Question
What assets require attention right now?

### Needs
- Asset status
- Asset location
- Rental status
- Utilization
- Idle hours
- Overdue rentals
- Alerts
- Recommendations

---

## 2. Site / Field Operator

### Main Question
What equipment is assigned to me and how do I record its usage?

### Needs
- Assigned equipment
- Check-in
- Check-out
- Usage logging
- Equipment status
- Issue reporting

---

## 3. Rental / Asset Planner

### Main Question
What equipment will be needed in the future?

### Needs
- Historical demand
- Forecast demand
- Equipment availability
- Site-level demand
- Repositioning recommendations
